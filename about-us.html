About us

About the society

Who we are, what we work for, and how we are registered.

Who we are

We are committed to empowering poor and disadvantaged communities to achieve sustainable development and secure their fundamental rights. The organization supports rural communities in their struggle against poverty, suffering, and social injustice, guided by compassion, dignity, and respect for human rights. A major focus of the Society is rescuing, rehabilitating, and reuniting mentally ill destitute individuals across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with their families, restoring hope and enabling dignified living through comprehensive rehabilitation.

The Society promotes sustainable and equitable development by ensuring that present needs are met without compromising the future, advocating for fair access to resources and opportunities. Key initiatives include promoting good agricultural practices, enhancing soil health, and building climate resilience to help communities adapt to environmental changes. The organization also encourages balanced nutrition and healthy lifestyles to improve overall well-being. To strengthen livelihood opportunities, it provides need-based skill development training to unorganized and unemployed youth, fostering economic independence and self-reliance.

Additionally, the Society actively works in disaster risk reduction by mapping community coping capacities, enhancing resilience among disaster-affected populations, improving early warning systems, securing livelihoods, raising public awareness, and advocating for strong and inclusive government policies for disaster preparedness and response.

Our mission

Our mission is to uplift vulnerable communities through compassionate mental health rescue and rehabilitation, sustainable rural development, and climate-resilient agricultural practices. We strive to promote nutrition and healthy living, empower youth with skills and livelihood opportunities, and strengthen disaster risk reduction and preparedness. Through inclusive, people-centered initiatives, we aim to create lasting well-being and opportunities for all.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a healthy, resilient, and self-reliant society where every individual has access to mental well-being, sustainable resources, and opportunities for growth. We aspire to create thriving rural communities equipped with climate-smart practices, empowered youth, improved nutrition, and strong disaster-ready systems ensuring a brighter, safer future for generations to come.
