About the society

Working with rural communities

We are committed to empowering poor and disadvantaged communities to achieve sustainable development and secure their fundamental rights. The organization supports rural communities in their struggle against poverty, suffering, and social injustice, guided by compassion, dignity, and respect for human rights. A major focus of the Society is rescuing, rehabilitating, and reuniting mentally ill destitute individuals across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with their families, restoring hope and enabling dignified living through comprehensive rehabilitation.

Our programmes

Six areas of work

Education for every child

Supporting children in rural families with learning and schooling.

Adult literacy and self-help groups

Reading and writing support for adults, and self-help groups that work together.

Library and knowledge sharing

Sharing knowledge, books and learning resources within the community.

Youth, sports and the arts

Skills training and activities that give young people new opportunities.

Nutrition for rural families

Promoting balanced food and healthy living for rural families.

Clean and green villages

Protecting the environment and building cleaner, greener villages.
