About us

Goutham Buddha Development Society is committed to empowering poor and disadvantaged communities to achieve sustainable development and secure their fundamental rights. The organization supports rural communities in their struggle against poverty, suffering, and social injustice, guided by compassion, dignity, and respect for human rights. A major focus of the Society is rescuing, rehabilitating, and reuniting mentally ill destitute individuals across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with their families, restoring hope and enabling dignified living through comprehensive rehabilitation.
